Who we are

A research companion, being built for how Africa actually researches.

The problem we saw

Good research ideas across Africa often stall — not from a lack of curiosity, but because reaching real respondents is expensive and hard to verify, and the analysis tools built for well-funded institutions elsewhere are out of reach here.

What we're building

MiRmiR is one platform designed to take a study from question to finding: verified respondents to answer it, affordable analytics to make sense of it, and a community to strengthen it along the way.

Verified data collection

ID-checked, incentivized respondents — so what you collect is trustworthy from the start.

Affordable analytics

Tools designed to be an accessible alternative to expensive software like SPSS.

Community & mentorship

A place for peer feedback and guidance, so no one is figuring out research methods alone.

Who it's for

Built for researchers first — useful to anyone who needs real answers.

Primary

Universities, researchers & students

Early-career researchers, students, and university staff who need affordable, verifiable ways to collect and analyze data.

Also welcome

Companies, NGOs & government

Organizations that need trustworthy, verified data from real people — for product research, program evaluation, or policy work.

Where we're starting: we're beginning conversations with universities in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia, with the hope of growing across Ethiopia and, over time, the wider East and Sub-Saharan Africa region.

What you gain

Built so both sides have a real reason to show up.

MiRmiR only works if it's genuinely worth researchers' and respondents' time — so here's what each side gets out of it.

If you're a researcher

  • Reach verified respondents without chasing contacts or guessing who really answered.
  • Spend less — affordable analytics instead of costly software licenses.
  • Get feedback from peers, so your study design improves before data collection starts.

If you're a respondent

  • Get paid for the time and knowledge you already have.
  • Build your profile once, and open the door to more matched opportunities over time.
  • Answer on your own schedule, and choose exactly what's visible about you.
You can be both. A student answering studies today might be posting their own thesis research tomorrow — one verified account, with a researcher profile and a respondent profile you can switch between, the way Upwork separates "find work" from "hire talent."
How it will work

Two sides, one simple exchange.

Researchers get verified answers. Respondents get paid for their time and knowledge. Here's the plan, as we're building it.

01

Post your study

Submit your questions and set the criteria for who you need to hear from.

02

Reach verified respondents

Get matched with people who fit your criteria, handpicked or filtered — each one ID-verified.

03

Collect and analyze

Responses land in your dataset ready for analysis, with tools built to make sense of them affordably.

01

Build your profile

Verify your ID once, add your background, and control exactly what's visible to researchers.

02

Get matched to studies

Apply to open studies or receive direct invitations that fit your profile.

03

Answer and get paid

Share your time and knowledge, then withdraw your earnings whenever you're ready.
